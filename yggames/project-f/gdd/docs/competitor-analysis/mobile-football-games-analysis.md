## 1. Score! Match

### 1.1 Overview
- **Developer:** First Touch Games Ltd.
- **Publisher:** First Touch Games Ltd.
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **Launch Date:** March 7, 2018
- **Downloads:** 100M+ (Google Play)
- **Google Play Rating:** 3.7/5 (1.19M reviews)
- **App Store Rating:** 4+ with 51,660+ ratings
- **Player Base:** Part of First Touch Games' ecosystem with 1 billion+ total downloads across all titles
- **Category:** Sports / PvP Soccer

### 1.2 Core Gameplay
Score! Match is a **real-time PvP soccer game** with a unique hybrid of direct control and AI-assisted play.

**Match Mechanics:**
- Matches are fast-paced, typically lasting a few minutes — ideal for mobile sessions
- Players use **swipe-based controls** to draw passing lanes, execute tackles, and take shots
- Rather than full 11v11 direct control, players manage the flow of play by selecting players, drawing paths, and timing actions
- The game uses **intelligent AI** for off-ball movement; the player intervenes at key moments
- Free kicks use trajectory-drawing swipe mechanics
- Real-time 1v1 multiplayer against global opponents
- Camera can be zoomed out/rotated for tactical awareness

**Game Modes:**
- **Arena Progression:** Advance through multiple arenas (tiers) on the way to the top
- **Seasons & Events:** Regular seasonal competitions and limited-time events with exclusive rewards
- **Grudge Matches:** Challenge friends directly in high-stakes matches
- **Friend Recruitment:** Recruit friends to join your team lineup

### 1.3 Card/Player System
Score! Match uses a **collectible card system** with diverse player archetypes:

**Player Types (16+ types):**
- **Defensive:** Guard (reliable CM), Commander (tall CB), Protector, Explorer, Infiltrator
- **Midfield:** Engine, Producer, Architect, Prowler
- **Attacking:** Speedster (fastest, lowest overall stats), Intruder (space exploiter), Menace, Hammer
- **Goalkeepers:** GK-Stopper, GK-Sweeper

**Super Players:**
- Available from Arena 3 onwards
- Divided into **Gold** and **Silver** classes
- Silver = direct upgrades to basic types
- Gold = unique playstyle that the whole team can be built around
- Each Super Player is unique to their type

**Upgrade System:**
- Collecting **duplicate player cards** allows leveling up
- Leveling unlocks improved stats and bonuses
- Players earn **packages** through arena progression, events, and matches
- Packages contain new player types, formations, boot designs, and cosmetics

**Formations:**
- Multiple formations available (e.g., 4-4-2 Counter, 3-5-2, etc.)
- Formations unlock through progression
- Strategic formation choice is critical — certain player types excel in specific positions

### 1.4 Monetization
- **Model:** Free-to-play with ads and IAP
- **Energy System:** Uses energy mechanics that gate play sessions, incentivizing either waiting or spending
- **In-App Purchases:** Packages and premium currency for faster progression
- **Ads:** Contains third-party advertising; rewarded ads likely available for bonus rewards
- **Randomized Content:** Card packs offered in randomized order based on displayed drop rates (loot box mechanics)
- **Cosmetics:** Boot designs, player skins available through packs
- **Revenue Model:** Hybrid (IAP + Ads), typical of mid-core mobile games

### 1.5 P2W Analysis
**Moderate P2W with significant balance concerns:**

- Spending accelerates player card collection and upgrades substantially
- Higher-level player cards provide tangible statistical advantages in matches
- Community reports suggest the game's **AI/matchmaking may be manipulated** — players report that when their win rate increases, the game's AI begins to "cheat" or handicap them
- The **Speedster long-pass strategy** reportedly has a ~90% success rate, suggesting balance issues beyond just spending
- Patient free-to-play players can progress, but impatient players face a less satisfying experience
- The randomized card pack system creates a **gacha-like progression barrier** where duplicates are needed for upgrades
- **Verdict:** Moderately P2W — spending gives clear advantages in squad quality, but real-time skill still matters in execution

### 1.6 Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- Fast, accessible matches perfect for mobile (2-3 minutes per match)
- Unique hybrid gameplay that combines swipe controls with tactical team management
- Deep player type system with meaningful strategic choices in team composition
- Real-time PvP creates genuine competitive tension
- Strong visual quality with 3D graphics and hundreds of unique animations
- Regular events and seasons keep content fresh
- Part of First Touch Games' trusted brand ecosystem
- Easy to learn, hard to master skill curve

**Weaknesses:**
- Persistent P2W complaints about matchmaking fairness
- Suspected **dynamic difficulty adjustment (DDA)** that punishes winning streaks
- Energy system limits play sessions for F2P players
- Rigid meta — certain strategies (Speedster long ball) dominate, reducing tactical diversity
- Card upgrade system creates a grind wall in mid-to-late game
- Lower Google Play rating (3.7) compared to competitors suggests accumulated player frustration
- Limited offline play (requires internet connection)
- Gameplay can feel repetitive once core loop is mastered

### 1.7 Player Retention & Churn Analysis
- **Genre advantage:** Match/sports games have the **highest D1 retention (32.6%)** and **best D30 retention (7.1%)** across all mobile game genres
- **Retention drivers:** Quick match length, real-time PvP excitement, collectible card progression, regular events/seasons
- **Churn drivers:**
  - Perceived unfair matchmaking and DDA push mid-level players away
  - Energy system frustrates engaged players who want to play more
  - Grind wall at higher arenas when F2P progression stalls
  - Meta staleness when dominant strategies emerge
  - Declining rating (3.7) suggests cumulative churn over the game's 8-year lifespan
- **Estimated retention profile:** Above-average D1 for sports games, but likely below-average D30 due to P2W frustration

## 2. Top Eleven

### 2.1 Overview
- **Developer:** Nordeus (Belgrade, Serbia)
- **Publisher:** Zynga / Take-Two Interactive (acquired Nordeus for $378M in February 2021)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, WebGL (Facebook), Microsoft Store, AppGallery, Aptoide
- **Launch Date:** May 9, 2010 (Facebook); October 2011 (Android); November 2011 (iOS)
- **Registered Users:** 300+ million
- **Revenue:** ~$75M annual revenue (as of 2025)
- **App Store/Play Store Rating:** Mixed reviews trending negative in recent years
- **Category:** Football Manager Simulation / RPG
- **Notable:** One of the **oldest and most successful** mobile football management games; 15+ years of continuous operation

### 2.2 Core Gameplay
Top Eleven is a **football club management simulation** where players act as managers making all strategic decisions.

**Management Mechanics:**
- **Tactics & Formation:** Set team formation, playing style, and tactical instructions
- **Training:** Train players to improve attributes; 2024 update revamped the training system
- **Match Day:** Matches happen in real-time with simulated results; players watch matches unfold based on tactical decisions
- **Seasons:** Compete across league seasons against other real managers
- **Quick decision-making** required as matches and events occur on real-world timelines

**Game Modes:**
- **League Competition:** Compete against other managers in tiered leagues
- **Cup Tournaments:** Knockout-style competitions
- **Association Play:** Join associations (guilds) for team-based challenges
- **Live Matches:** Watch tactical decisions play out in real-time simulations
- **Campus (2026):** New club-building feature with expanded customization for stadiums and facilities
- **Fan Loyalty (2026):** Engage fan base to boost player performance

**2026 Update Features:**
- "Grounds" section revamped with more customization and daily engagement activities
- "Campus" — reimagined club-building mechanic with huge content expansions
- Fan Loyalty — performing well in matches and competing for trophies engages fans who boost superstar performance

### 2.3 Card/Player System
**Player Attributes & Development:**
- Players have detailed attribute systems (speed, strength, technique, etc.)
- Player **condition/fitness** system — rest is required, and low condition leads to poor performance and injury risk
- Rest system: Each rest improves condition by 15%; "Express Rest" requires Rest Boosters (formula: signed players / 3)

**Youth Academy / Scouting:**
- Scout and sign players through the transfer market
- Youth development pipeline for growing talent

**Transfer Market:**
- **Auction system** where managers bid on players using Tokens (premium currency)
- Player negotiations and direct offers from other managers
- Cash earned from selling players (separate from Token economy)

**Special Players:**
- Players with special abilities can be purchased
- Higher-quality players available through premium spending

### 2.4 Monetization
**Premium Currency: Tokens**
- Core premium currency used for: bidding on auctions, purchasing players, hiring trainers, speeding up facility construction, purchasing health/rest packs
- Can be earned F2P via: TV Rights sponsorships (1 token/day), player negotiations, association rewards (Silver 1+), daily login bonuses, watching ads, completing offers
- Can be purchased with real money

**Additional Monetization:**
- **Rest Packs:** Health/rest boosters for player condition management
- **Special Sponsors:** Premium sponsorship deals
- **Credits:** For purchasing high-level players
- **Special Trainers:** Paid trainers for accelerated player development
- **Monthly Cards:** Subscription-style packages for increased gold/currency
- **Ad-driven events:** February 2026 review notes "all events are now geared towards watching ads or paying for premium sponsors"
- **Manager Offers:** Targeted IAP bundles

**Revenue Streams:** Tokens (IAP), ad revenue, subscription-like monthly cards, event-driven spending

### 2.5 P2W Analysis
**Strongly P2W with vocal community backlash:**

Top Eleven has one of the most documented P2W controversies in mobile football gaming:

- **Forum threads titled "The worst pay to win game ever"** with 58+ replies and 30+ likes — a multi-page community discussion
- Players report being placed in leagues against **teams 40-60% stronger** — non-spenders can never realistically compete for titles
- A June 2025 review states the game's **algorithms are set against players unless willing to pay upwards of GBP 20/month**
- A January 2025 review claims developers are "looking for every possible way to extract money"
- February 2026 review states the game is "far too ad driven" and all events are geared toward ads/premium spending

**P2W Mechanics:**
- Token-based auction system means spenders can always outbid F2P for top players
- Rest/health packs create a **pay-to-maintain** model — your squad literally degrades without spending
- Special trainers and ability players locked behind premium spending
- Speed-up mechanics for facility construction reward impatience with money

**Counter-argument (from community):**
- Some argue it's not "classic" P2W because non-payers aren't entirely eliminated — they can still play
- The game doesn't have a hard paywall; F2P can earn tokens slowly
- Strategic play and tactical knowledge can partially offset squad quality gaps

**Verdict:** Heavily P2W in competitive contexts. While casual play is possible F2P, competing for league titles and cups without spending is effectively impossible at higher levels.

### 2.6 Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- **Longevity & brand recognition** — 15+ years of operation, 300M+ registered users
- Deep management simulation with tactics, training, transfers, and facilities
- Strong social features (associations, friend challenges, real-time competition)
- Multi-platform availability (mobile, web, Facebook, Microsoft Store)
- Regular annual updates with meaningful new features (Campus, Fan Loyalty in 2026)
- Real-time match simulation creates engagement and anticipation
- Backed by Take-Two/Zynga resources ensuring long-term support
- Massive global community creating network effects

**Weaknesses:**
- **Aggressive and escalating monetization** alienating long-term players
- P2W perception is severe and well-documented, especially in competitive leagues
- Ad-driven events replacing skill-based progression (2025-2026 trend)
- Condition/rest system feels punitive — essentially a "pay-to-maintain" tax
- Aging core systems despite annual cosmetic updates
- Matchmaking criticized for systematically pairing F2P against spenders
- Player trust eroding — reviews increasingly negative in 2024-2026 period
- Complex economy (tokens, cash, rest packs, credits) can overwhelm new players

### 2.7 Player Retention & Churn Analysis

**Retention Drivers:**
- Seasonal structure creates natural re-engagement hooks
- Association/social features build community bonds
- 15-year legacy creates habitual daily players
- Real-time match pacing creates appointment-style engagement (check back for results)
- Deep management simulation satisfies core genre fans
- Annual updates provide "new season" excitement

**Churn Drivers:**
- P2W frustration is the **#1 reported reason** for quitting across forums and reviews
- Escalating monetization pressure (ads, premium events) accelerating in 2024-2026
- Power gap between spenders and F2P widens over time, creating hopelessness
- Long-term players report the game "isn't fair anymore" after 7+ years
- Condition/rest system creates daily maintenance burden — missing days has real penalties
- New player onboarding is increasingly difficult against established managers

**Retention Profile:**
- Very high D1-D7 retention due to management sim engagement loops
- Strong D30+ retention among paying players
- Severe D90+ churn among F2P players who hit competitive ceilings
- 15-year operational history suggests strong whale retention but declining average user base quality

## 3. Dream League Soccer (DLS)

### 3.1 Overview
- **Developer:** First Touch Games Ltd.
- **Publisher:** SEGA (as of recent editions)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **Current Version:** Dream League Soccer 2026
- **Downloads:** 100M+ (Google Play)
- **Google Play Rating:** 4.0/5 (13.6M reviews)
- **Monthly Active Users:** 20+ million
- **Category:** Sports / Soccer Simulation
- **Licensing:** FIFPRO licensed (4,000+ real players)
- **Notable:** One of the most popular mobile football games globally; the sibling title to Score! Match from the same developer

### 3.2 Core Gameplay
DLS delivers a **full 3D football simulation** designed for touchscreen, combining arcade accessibility with simulation depth.

**Match Gameplay:**
- Full match control with on-screen virtual buttons (pass, shoot, tackle, sprint, through ball, lob)
- **3D motion-captured animations** from real athletes for kicks, passes, tackles, and celebrations
- Immersive in-game commentary
- Improved player AI in 2026 edition
- Realistic passing mechanics with physics-based ball movement
- Smoother animations in DLS 2026 compared to previous editions

**Controls:**
- Intuitive touchscreen controls — easy to learn for newcomers
- Some reported issues: player switching can have 0.5-2 second delay; controls and player switching not always synced
- Arcade-style blended with realism — less tactical depth than console games but more accessible

**Game Modes:**
- **Career Mode:** Build a club from scratch — sign players, upgrade stadium, customize kits, compete across divisions; the core experience and primary draw
- **Multiplayer:** Online competitive matches against other players; described as "highly competitive"
- **Offline Play:** Career mode available offline — a major differentiator vs. competitors
- **Clans (2026 addition):** Form or join clans for team-based challenges, leaderboards, and exclusive rewards
- **Seasons/Events:** Regular content updates reflecting real-world transfers
- **Dream Stars 26:** Enhanced player cards with special appearances
- **12th Man Vote:** Community-driven player picks

### 3.3 Card/Player System
**Player Recruitment:**
- Over 4,000 FIFPRO-licensed football players available
- Players recruited through in-game currency (coins) or premium currency (gems/diamonds)
- Legendary players (e.g., Pele) available as premium/special content
- Dream Stars 26 — enhanced card system with improved visual appearances
- Agent system for player recruitment (community advises against spending gems on agents)

**Player Development:**
- Players can be upgraded and developed over time
- Training programs (including premium training programs) accelerate development
- Stadium, commercial, and medical facilities can be upgraded
- Player ratings matter significantly — 90+ rated players locked behind premium currency

**Progression System:**
- Start with a small, unknown club and work upward through divisions
- Stadium upgrades with world-class facilities
- Kit and logo customization — massive community around custom designs
- Season Pass system with free and premium tiers

### 3.4 Monetization
**Dual Currency System:**

| Currency | Earn Rate | Primary Use |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Coins** | Abundant — matches, Season Pass (1,095 coins at 400 points), ads (30-40 coins per ad) | Player signing, day-to-day operations |
| **Gems/Diamonds** | Scarce — login bonuses (every 20 days), limited free sources | Premium players (90+ rated), stadium/facility upgrades, premium content |

**Monetization Channels:**
- **In-App Purchases:** Gems/diamonds purchasable with real money
- **Ads:** Rewarded video ads for coins (30-40 per ad); third-party display ads
- **Season Pass:** Free tier with coin rewards; likely premium tier for additional content
- **Login Bonuses:** 20-day refresh cycle with mix of coins and occasional gems

**Spending Advice from Community:**
- Coins should be used for player signing, coaches, and physios
- Gems should primarily go to facility upgrades (Stadium, Commercial, Medical)
- Avoid spending gems on Agents — considered a waste of resources
- Patience allows effective F2P play, but premium currency unlocks the best players

### 3.5 P2W Analysis
**Mildly P2W — mostly skill-based with progression advantages for spenders:**

- DLS adopts a **"relatively fair" F2P model** per multiple reviews — non-paying players can compete effectively
- Core gameplay is **skill-based** — touchscreen mechanics, tactical decisions, and player skill determine match outcomes more than squad quality alone
- However, gems/diamonds create a meaningful gap:
  - **90+ rated players are effectively premium-locked** — "without diamonds, players are essentially playing with one hand tied behind their back"
  - Premium players unlock exclusive kits, training programs, and stadium upgrades
  - Spending accelerates progression significantly
- **Offline career mode** is entirely unaffected by P2W — you play at your own pace
- Online multiplayer is where spending matters, but skill still trumps squad quality to a significant degree
- The game earns a **9.2/10 review rating** from comprehensive reviews, suggesting monetization isn't severely damaging the experience

**Verdict:** Mildly P2W. Skill matters more than spending in actual gameplay. The premium currency primarily accelerates progression and unlocks top-tier roster players, but F2P players can build competitive teams through patience. The offline career mode is fully accessible without spending.

### 3.6 Strengths and Weaknesses

**Strengths:**
- **Best-in-class mobile football gameplay** — intuitive controls with simulation depth
- Deep career mode with satisfying long-term progression (small club to champion)
- **Offline play support** — massive differentiator for mobile gaming
- Stunning 3D graphics with motion-captured animations (rated 9.5/10 for visuals)
- Extensive customization (kits, logos, stadiums) creating a passionate community
- FIFPRO licensing with 4,000+ real players and transfer window updates
- Relatively fair F2P model — skill matters more than spending
- 20M+ monthly active users indicating strong market position
- High Google Play rating (4.0 with 13.6M reviews) — exceptional volume
- Clan system (2026) adding social/cooperative dimensions
- Published by SEGA, ensuring strong distribution and support

**Weaknesses:**
- **Player switching delays** (0.5-2 seconds) frustrate competitive players
- AI/goalkeeper behavior can be inconsistent and exploitable
- Striker AI sometimes steps back when attacking; CB tackling issues
- Can be demanding on lower-end devices — performance optimization gaps
- Gems/diamonds create a real gap for top-tier players despite overall fairness
- Learning curve may be steep for very casual players
- Some frame rate drops and lag during matches
- Controls not as polished as console counterparts (inevitable mobile limitation)
- Career mode can become repetitive once top division is reached

### 3.7 Player Retention & Churn Analysis

**Retention Drivers:**
- Career mode progression provides long-term goals (building a dynasty)
- Offline play means players aren't lost during connectivity issues
- Regular content updates (transfer windows, seasonal content, Dream Stars)
- Kit/stadium customization creates personal investment and community engagement
- Clan system (2026) adds social retention hooks
- Accessible skill floor but high skill ceiling keeps both casual and hardcore players
- 20M MAU demonstrates proven retention capability

**Churn Drivers:**
- Mid-to-late career mode can feel repetitive once top division is dominated
- AI inconsistencies frustrate competitive players seeking reliable challenge
- Control/switching delays cause rage-quit moments
- Premium currency scarcity can create frustration for F2P players wanting top players
- Performance issues on lower-end devices exclude a segment of the market
- Annual edition releases (DLS 2025 to 2026) require re-engagement; some players don't return

**Retention Profile:**
- Excellent D1 retention due to immediate gameplay satisfaction
- Strong D7-D30 retention driven by career mode progression hooks
- Above-average D90+ retention among career mode enthusiasts
- Clan system in 2026 likely improves long-term social retention
- Risk of yearly edition fatigue as players must restart or migrate

## 4. Other Notable Mobile Football Games

### 4.1 EA SPORTS FC Mobile (formerly FIFA Mobile)
- **Developer:** EA Sports
- **Downloads:** 500M+ (most downloaded mobile football game)
- **Key Features:** 19,000+ players from 690 teams across 35 leagues including UEFA Champions League; Ultimate Team mode; Division Rivals; Head-to-Head matches; seasonal live events
- **Monetization:** Heavily IAP-driven with card pack mechanics (Ultimate Team model); aggressive monetization
- **P2W Level:** High — Ultimate Team mechanics create strong P2W dynamics
- **Strengths:** Massive licensing power, polished presentation, frequent content updates, large player base
- **Weaknesses:** Heavy P2W, repetitive event-driven grind, pay-to-progress card economy

### 4.2 eFootball (formerly PES Mobile)
- **Developer:** Konami
- **Rating:** Highest-rated football game on Android
- **Key Features:** Console-quality realistic gameplay, tactical depth, official licenses (fewer than EA but growing), skill-based competitive play
- **Monetization:** F2P with IAP; less aggressive than FC Mobile
- **Strengths:** Most realistic mobile football gameplay, strong tactical mechanics, less P2W than competitors
- **Weaknesses:** Steep learning curve, fewer licenses than EA, occasional server issues

### 4.3 Total Football
- **Developer:** Unknown (emerging title)
- **Launch:** November 3, 2025
- **Performance:** 10M+ downloads within weeks; 4.3 rating on Google Play
- **Key Features:** 4,000+ licensed players including Ronaldo, Messi, Haaland; quick rise to popularity
- **Notable:** Rapid growth suggests strong market demand for new mobile football entrants

### 4.4 Football Manager Mobile (FM26 Mobile)
- **Developer:** Sports Interactive (via Netflix on mobile)

- **Issues:** Luck-based gameplay complaints; matchmaking pairs low-level with high-level opponents; ad reward issues
- **P2W Level:** Moderate — progression advantages for spenders but gameplay is largely skill/luck based

### 4.6 Retro Goal
- **Developer:** New Star Games

- **Key Features:** 16-bit retro-style arcade soccer; swipe-based shooting; light team management between matches; career progression

- **P2W Level:** Minimal — very light monetization

### 4.7 Soccer Manager 2026

- **Monetization:** F2P with IAP
- **Rating:** Mixed — interface improvements praised but gameplay quality questioned

### 4.8 UFL (United Football League)
- **Developer:** Strikerz Inc.
- **Launch:** January 2025 (mobile version)
- **Key Features:** Positioned as a "fair-to-play" alternative to EA FC; licensed players
- **Notable:** Aims to differentiate by reducing P2W mechanics

### 4.9 Football League 2026
- **Key Features:** 40,000+ players (men & women), 1,500 clubs, 150 national teams; fully licensed including Manchester City and AS Monaco
- **Notable:** Strong licensing breadth; stylish UI with 3D player action

## 7. Strategic Insights for Project F

### What Works Across Successful Titles
1. **Skill-based gameplay** retains players longer than P2W-heavy models (DLS vs Top Eleven retention)
2. **Offline play** is a massive mobile differentiator (DLS's key advantage)
3. **Quick match lengths** (2-5 minutes) suit mobile play patterns (Score! Match's strength)
4. **Career/progression modes** create long-term engagement hooks (DLS career mode)
5. **Social features** (clans, associations) improve D30+ retention significantly
6. **Fair monetization** earns higher ratings and better word-of-mouth (DLS 4.0 vs Score Match 3.7)
7. **Regular content updates** tied to real-world football events maintain relevance

### What Drives Players Away
1. **Aggressive P2W** is the #1 complaint across Top Eleven, Score! Match, and EA FC Mobile
2. **Dynamic difficulty adjustment (DDA)** suspected in Score! Match destroys trust
3. **Pay-to-maintain** mechanics (Top Eleven rest system) feel punitive
4. **Gacha/randomized progression** creates frustration and negative sentiment
5. **Escalating ad pressure** (Top Eleven 2025-2026 trend) alienates established players
6. **Unfair matchmaking** pairing F2P against whales causes immediate churn

### Monetization Sweet Spot
- DLS 2026 demonstrates the most sustainable model: coins abundantly available through gameplay, premium currency (gems) for acceleration/cosmetics but not gatekeeping core content
- Season Pass model (free + premium tier) is well-received industry-wide
- Rewarded ads at 62% of ad revenue and 45-60% engagement suggest players accept voluntary ad viewing
- Avoid: energy systems (Score! Match), token-gated auctions (Top Eleven), mandatory ad events (Top Eleven 2026)
