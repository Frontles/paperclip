## 2. Core Gameplay

### 2.1 Game Modes (Division Rivals)

**VS Attack (VSA)**
- Asynchronous turn-based PvP mode
- Players only play offensive phases (attacking chances)
- Difficulty of chances determined by team OVR differential
- Quick matches (~2-3 minutes)
- Lower event token rewards compared to H2H

**Head to Head (H2H)**
- Full 11v11 real-time PvP match
- Manual player control: dribbling, passing, shooting, defending
- Sped-up 90-minute match (~6-8 minutes real time)
- Tied scores do not go to extra time (except in Tournaments)
- Awards double event tokens vs VSA/MM
- Improved ranking system and matchmaking in FC Mobile 26

**Manager Mode (MM)**
- Tactical/AFK mode — AI plays out your strategy
- Focus on formation, tactics, and squad composition rather than manual skill
- Only mode that shows player performance ratings post-match
- Community reports "scripting" concerns at higher star levels (15-20 stars)

**VS Friends**
- Play VSA or H2H matches against friends

### 2.2 Controls & Gameplay Improvements (FC Mobile 26)
- Smarter defender AI — faster reactions in Kickoff Rush, reducing easy opening goals
- Long balls rebalanced — attributes, speed mismatches, and positioning matter more
- Lofted through passes — more accurate with high-attribute passers, harder at longer distances
- Cross accuracy depends more on attributes and defensive pressure
- Reduced "auto-foul" — referees let the game flow better, fewer cheap free kicks
- Seven new formations added: 4-2-1-3, 4-1-3-2, 3-4-2-1, and more
- Pre/post match cutscenes for broadcast-like immersion
- Faster, more agile dribbling with improved responsiveness

### 2.3 Other Modes
- **Leagues:** Join/create leagues, play League Tournaments (LvL), league chat
- **Tournaments:** PvP bracket-style competition
- **Football Centre:** Connects to real-world football events and live match tie-ins
- **Daily Challenge, Star Scout, Extra Time, Scouting Camp, Hero Chronicles, Club Challenge:** Permanent daily/weekly PvE content
- **Weekend Challenge:** Saturday & Sunday special events

## 3. Card/Player System

### 3.1 OVR System
- **Base OVR:** Each player card has a base Overall Rating
- **Team OVR formula:** Average Base OVR (rounded up) + Average Rank (rounded up)
- OVR directly impacts VSA chance difficulty and matchmaking

### 3.2 Training System
- Melt 1+ players into another to increase stats (not OVR)
- Training goes up to Level 30 without needing any rank
- No coin cost for training in current season — only player cards as Training XP (TXP)

### 3.3 Rank Up System
- Uses position-specific Rank Up Items to increase OVR
- Every 5 Training Levels = eligible for 1 Rank Up (+1 OVR)
- Maximum 5 Rank Ups per player (e.g., 80 OVR → 85 OVR)
- Each rank grants 1 Skill Point (5 total at max rank)

### 3.4 Chemistry System
- Players from same Club, League, or Nationality give Chemistry Points to linked players
- Total Team Chemistry impacts performance in VSA and H2H
- Prioritize rank-ups on players with strong chemistry links
- Chemistry encourages building themed/coherent squads rather than random all-star teams

### 3.5 Player Tiers & Card Types
- **Base Cards:** Standard player cards from packs/events
- **Event Cards:** Higher-rated cards from seasonal events (TOTY, TOTS, UCL, etc.)
- **ICONs:** Legendary players (Zlatan, Xabi Alonso, Marcelo, Beckham, etc.)
- **Heroes:** Special hero player cards with unique attributes
- **TOTW (Team of the Week):** Weekly updated cards based on real-world performance
- **Promotional Cards:** Event-specific higher-tier cards (Thunderstruck, Record Breakers, etc.)

### 3.6 Power Creep Pattern
- OVR ceiling rises throughout the season as new events release higher-rated cards
- Early season base cards start ~75-85 OVR
- End-of-season event cards reach 100+ OVR
- Creates constant pressure to acquire new cards to remain competitive
- Players who return after months find their squads severely outdated

## 4. Monetization

### 4.1 FC Points (Premium Currency)
| Points | Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| 40 | $0.39 |
| 100 | ~$0.99 |
| Larger bundles | Scaling up |
| 12,000 | $99.99 |

- Larger bundles offer better per-point value
- Prices vary slightly by region and platform (iOS vs Android)
- FC Points used to purchase packs, Star Pass, and premium content
- FC Points sales banned in Belgium due to gambling classification

### 4.2 Star Pass (Monthly Battle Pass)
- **Free Tier:** Available to all players with basic rewards
- **Premium Star Pass:** 999 Silver (~$11.59 USD)
- **Premium Star Pass Bundle:** 1,999 Silver (~$23.19 USD) — includes 6,000 Star Pass Credits for faster progression
- **Content:** Coins, Gems, FC Points, Training Transfer Points, cosmetics (Emotes, Kits)
- **Event integration:** Premium tier includes exclusive event currencies
- **Progression:** Earn up to 1,000 Star Pass Credits daily + 1,400 weekly bonus
- **Cadence:** New Star Pass every month, themed around current events

### 4.3 Event Passes
- Major events (TOTY, TOTS, UCL, etc.) often have their own purchasable passes/packs
- Event-specific currencies sometimes gated behind premium purchases
- Limited-time bundles with guaranteed high-OVR players

### 4.4 Pack Odds Disclosure
- EA displays pack probabilities in-store via "More Pack Info" button
- Probabilities grouped by OVR and rarity categories
- Each pack opening is independent (not cumulative)
- 100% categories guarantee at least one item from that tier
- Probabilities calculated mathematically and validated via large-scale simulations
- Updated with every content update
- **Criticism:** Odds shown in broad ranges (e.g., 75+, 82+, 88+) rather than individual rating tiers — obscures true probability of top-tier pulls

### 4.5 Other Monetization
- Direct player/pack purchases in store
- Cosmetic items (kits, celebrations, emotes)
- EA webstore (store.fcm.ea.com) for direct FC Points purchases

## 5. Pay-to-Win (P2W) Analysis

### How Much Does Spending Matter?
- **Acceleration, not gating:** Spending primarily accelerates progression rather than providing exclusive content entirely unavailable to F2P
- **OVR advantage:** P2W players achieve higher OVR faster, which directly impacts VSA chance difficulty (easier chances for higher OVR)
- **Event completion:** Premium Star Pass and event passes allow faster/fuller event completion, yielding higher-rated cards
- **Pack luck:** Premium packs offer chances at top-tier cards earlier, though odds remain low even for paying players

### Can F2P Players Compete?
- **Yes, with caveats:** F2P players can build competitive teams through consistent daily play, smart resource management, and event grinding
- **Time vs money tradeoff:** What takes a P2W player days can take F2P weeks or months
- **Competitive ceiling:** In high-level Division Rivals and leaderboards, P2W players dominate due to OVR advantage
- **Research finding:** Academic research confirms time played is a better predictor of achievement than money spent, but spending still significantly contributes to rankings and perceived competence

### The Gap
- Early season: Gap is large — P2W players have 10-15+ OVR advantage
- Mid season: Gap narrows as F2P catch up through events
- Late season: Gap widens again as new premium events release even higher-rated cards
- **Estimated P2W advantage:** A player spending $50-100/month maintains a consistent 5-10 OVR edge over dedicated F2P
- **Whales (heavy spenders):** Can maintain 15-20+ OVR advantage, making VSA nearly unwinnable for F2P opponents

## 6. Season/Reset Mechanics

### Historical Pattern (2016-2024)
- Annual full reset every September/October with new "season" launch
- All player cards, coins, progress wiped
- Players received "Legacy" versions of select players as compensation
- Legacy players typically low-rated and quickly obsolete
- Reset was the single most controversial feature — drove significant player churn

### 2025 Major Shift: No Season Reset
- **EA officially announced in June 2025:** "There will be no Season Reset in 2025"
- FC Mobile 26 update (September 2025) was a seamless continuation rather than full wipe
- Players retained all cards, currencies, and progress
- First time in game history with no reset

### Community Reaction to No-Reset
- **Overwhelmingly positive:** Players celebrated retaining their squads
- **New play patterns emerged:** Some players shifted to building theme squads and enjoying unranked matches rather than chasing OVR
- **Concern about 2026:** Community speculation that a reset may return in 2026 with the next TOTY event
- **Retention impact:** Removing the annual wipe addressed the #1 complaint driving player churn

### Legacy Player System (Historical)
- At reset, players could keep a few "Legacy" player cards
- Legacy cards were downgraded versions, typically 70-80 OVR
- Meant as a "thank you" but widely seen as inadequate compensation for months of grinding/spending
- Created cynicism: "Why invest if everything gets wiped?"

## 7. Player Retention & Churn

### What Keeps Players
- **Licensing authenticity:** Real players, teams, leagues — unmatched in mobile football
- **Event cadence:** New event every 1-2 weeks keeps content fresh
- **Squad building satisfaction:** Collecting and upgrading favorite players
- **Competitive PvP:** Division Rivals progression and leaderboards
- **Social features:** Leagues, league tournaments, VS Friends
- **Real-world connection:** Football Centre ties to live football calendar
- **Sunk cost:** Players invested months/years don't want to abandon progress

### What Drives Players Away
- **Annual resets (historically):** The #1 churn driver — wiping progress destroyed motivation
- **Scripting perception:** Widespread belief that AI manipulates match outcomes to force losses
- **Power creep fatigue:** Constant need to upgrade cards feels like a treadmill
- **P2W frustration:** F2P players hitting walls against spenders
- **Bugs and technical issues:** Lag, ping spikes, unresponsive controls, login failures
- **OVR obsession:** Gameplay becomes more about numbers than fun
- **Repetitive daily tasks:** Grinding same content daily for incremental rewards burns out players

### Community Sentiment (2025-2026)
- **Reddit r/FUTMobile:** Active community (~100K+ members), mix of appreciation and frustration
- **EA Forums:** Significant scripting complaints, bug reports, and feedback threads
- **General trend:** FC Mobile 26 update received cautiously positive reception for gameplay improvements; no-reset decision was major goodwill boost

## 9. Weaknesses & Mistakes

### 9.1 Annual Reset Controversy (2016-2024)
- For 8 years, full progress wipes alienated large portions of the player base
- Players who spent hundreds/thousands of dollars saw investments evaporate annually
- Created a "why bother?" mentality that suppressed both spending and engagement
- Finally addressed in 2025, but damage to trust was already done for many players
- Likely contributed to lower lifetime revenue vs. potential

### 9.2 Scripting / Dynamic Difficulty Complaints
- Persistent, widespread player belief that EA manipulates match outcomes
- Reports of high-rated squads underperforming against weaker teams
- Claims of "progression checks" in Manager Mode at 15-20 star range
- EA denies scripting, but community distrust is deeply embedded
- EA was granted a patent for "Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment" (DDA) in 2018, fueling suspicions
- Creates frustration and perception that skill/investment don't matter

### 9.3 Power Creep
- Relentless OVR inflation throughout each season
- Cards purchased/earned in January are obsolete by June
- Creates a treadmill effect — players must continuously grind/spend to stay competitive
- Disincentivizes attachment to specific players since they'll be replaced
- Heavy spenders report "nerfed" squads — high-rated teams feeling sluggish

### 9.4 Technical Issues
- Persistent bugs: lag spikes, high ping (especially in last 85-90 min of matches), login failures
- Missing rewards from events
- Account suspension issues
- Frame rate locked at 30fps on many devices despite hardware capability
- Graphics settings limitations frustrate players with high-end phones (120fps phones can't exceed "standard")

### 9.5 Pack Odds Transparency
- Odds disclosed in broad ranges rather than per-rating probabilities
- Top-tier player probabilities often listed as "<1%" — could mean 0.9% or 0.001%
- Creates perception of deceptive practices
- Regulatory pressure mounting (Belgium ban, Netherlands legal challenges, South Korea disclosure laws)

### 9.6 Monetization Aggressiveness
- Constant pop-up offers and "limited time" bundles create purchase pressure
- Event currencies sometimes require premium purchases to fully complete events
- Gap between F2P and P2W grows during major events

## 10. Sustainability Analysis

### Positive Indicators
- **Revenue growth:** Record net bookings in FY2026 Q1; double-digit YoY growth
- **User growth:** DAU and new player acquisition up 20%+ YoY
- **No-reset decision:** Signals EA is listening to community and willing to change core mechanics for retention
- **Content pipeline:** Massive licensing portfolio ensures content depth for years
- **EA investment:** Mobile is 15% of EA revenue ($1.1B); company is committed to the platform
- **Global reach:** Regional publishers (Tencent, Nexon, Garena) expand reach in key Asian markets

### Risk Factors
- **Regulatory threat:** Loot box legislation expanding globally could force monetization model changes
- **Competition:** eFootball (Konami), UFL, and emerging competitors challenging EA's mobile dominance
- **Player fatigue:** Core gameplay loop hasn't fundamentally changed since 2016
- **Trust deficit:** Years of resets, scripting concerns, and P2W perception have eroded goodwill
- **2026 reset uncertainty:** If EA reintroduces resets, it could trigger massive churn from players who returned/stayed because of the no-reset promise
- **Power creep unsustainability:** Continuous OVR inflation eventually makes numbers meaningless and requires either a reset or total restructure
- **Platform dependency:** App store policy changes (Apple/Google commission cuts, gambling regulation) could impact profitability

### Long-Term Outlook
FC Mobile is in a strong commercial position with growing revenue and users, bolstered by EA's unmatched licensing portfolio. The no-reset decision in 2025 was a pivotal retention strategy. However, the game faces a structural tension between monetization (which relies on power creep and FOMO-driven spending) and long-term retention (which requires players to feel their investment is respected). The sustainability of the current model depends on EA's ability to maintain content freshness without alienating players through aggressive monetization or returning to destructive reset cycles.

## 11. FIFA Ultimate Team (FUT) — Console/PC Reference

### Overview
- Game mode within EA Sports FC (console/PC) since FIFA 09 (2008)
- Described as "the most important strand of the globe-spanning mega-franchise"
- Revenue: ~$1.6-1.7B annually from Ultimate Team modes across all EA Sports titles
- Cumulative: $6B+ since 2015; $7B+ total lifetime
- Accounts for ~75% of EA's live services revenue

### Key Mechanics Relevant to Mobile Card Games

**Pack System**
- Randomized player packs purchased with coins (earned) or FC Points (purchased)
- Pack odds disclosed but criticized as insufficient (broad ranges, not per-rating)
- Pack opening is a major content/streaming category — drives visibility
- Intermittent reinforcement schedules (slot machine psychology)

**Chemistry System**
- Players from same club/league/nation provide chemistry bonuses
- "Cornerstones" in FC 26 provide permanent upgrades and +2 club chemistry links
- Chemistry incentivizes diverse squad building rather than just picking highest-rated players

**Transfer Market**
- Real-time player auction house (not present in FC Mobile)
- Players buy/sell cards, creating a virtual economy
- Market manipulation and trading are a meta-game in themselves
- Enables F2P players to "trade their way up"

**Squad Building Challenges (SBCs)**
- Submit squads meeting specific requirements to earn rewards
- Creates a "card sink" — removes cards from circulation to manage economy
- Scaling difficulty throughout the season
- Encourages engagement with squad building beyond just matches

**Evolutions (FC 26)**
- Personalized upgrade paths for player items
- More accessible ways to improve cards without relying solely on packs
- Gives players more control and agency over progression

### Regulatory History
- **Belgium (2018):** Gaming Commission classified loot boxes as gambling; EA stopped selling FIFA Points in Belgium
- **Netherlands (2020):** Court initially agreed FUT packs = gambling; later overturned by Council of State (not a "stand-alone game of chance")
- **South Korea (2023-2024):** New law requiring loot box odds disclosure; EA disabled Ultimate Team packs for Korean users in FC 26
- **UK:** Gambling Commission does not currently classify FUT packs as gambling but recommends more transparency
- **Academic research:** Problem loot box use is more closely related to gambling addiction than video game addiction
- **EA's position:** Describes Ultimate Team as "predominantly a game of skill"; states only 1 in 10 players buys packs with real money

### Console FUT vs Mobile FC Differences
| Feature | FUT (Console/PC) | FC Mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Transfer Market | Yes (real-time auction) | No (removed in mobile) |
| SBCs | Yes (extensive) | Limited/Exchange system |
| Match Length | Full 12-min matches | 2-8 min depending on mode |
| Chemistry | Complex with Cornerstones | Simplified club/league/nation |
| Controls | Full controller input | Touch controls + auto-play |
| Annual Reset | New game purchase ($60-70) | Free update (no reset in 2025) |
| Pack Odds | Disclosed (broad ranges) | Disclosed (broad ranges) |
| Price | $60-70 base game | Free-to-play |
